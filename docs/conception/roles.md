**Roles**

**Setup**

- **Captain Selection**
  - The Captain is determined by real money **auction**.
  - The highest bidder receives the Captain role.
  - If no one bids, the role is granted randomly among volunteers (yes/no option at start). If no one volunteers, the role is granted randomly.
- **Chaplain Selection**
  - Selection is opt-in (“yes” or “no” to volunteering).
  - Among all “yes” volunteers, the one with the **highest honor** becomes Chaplain.
  - If no one volunteers, the role is granted randomly.
  - Chaplain is never a cultist.
- **All Other Roles**
  - Players indicate their **preferences** among the remaining roles.
  - The system tries to maximize preference matches for all players.
  - When preferences overlap, higher preference is respected first.
  - If two or more players have equal preference for the same role, assignment between them is random.
  - Players who express no preference are assigned randomly.
- **Cultist Assignment**
  - Once roles are locked in, the game randomly assigns two cultists among the crew.
  - Only restriction: the Chaplain can never be chosen as cultist.

Here is why choosing different mental loads is satisfying in *Northwest*:

- **Freedom of engagement** – players can decide if they want a high-pressure role (Captain, Chaplain) or a calmer, reactive one (Marine, Brute).
- **Replay variety** – alternating between talk-heavy, responsibility-laden roles and quieter, tactical ones keeps the game fresh across sessions.
- **Personality fit** – extroverts can lean into leadership or inquiry, while introverts can contribute meaningfully through lower-stress support roles.
- **Shifting perspectives** – playing both high-load and low-load roles helps players understand how others think and act, enriching the social layer.
- **Dynamic crew composition** – each game feels different depending on who takes the heavy-load seats, making every voyage unique.

**Captain**
Category: Inquirer and support producer

Starting items: Sword, Pistol and Prison Key

**Capabilities**

- Wields the most accurate rogue scan
- Exclusive actions provide increased efficiency by directing other players’ actions and a modest restoration of hope.

**Player Experience: Influence and polyvalence**

His scans often make him a prominent actor in trust debates while his control over the brig and his weapons grant him substantial influence over the conclusions of those debates.  Any TP not spent on scans must be carefully spent on his exclusive production actions. Playing him means constant visibility and impactful decisions.

**Chaplain**

Category**:** Elite inquirer but vulnerable fighter
**Capabilities**

- Cannot be an acolyte.
- Exclusive actions provide identity and rogue scans and hope restorations
- Weakest fighter and cannot initiate attacks except against revealed acolytes.

**Player Experience: Pillar of trust**

The Chaplain is both central and exposed. Everyone confides in him, including acolytes trying to bend suspicion elsewhere. He must balance scarce hope-restoring actions with inquiry, all while surviving as the weakest fighter on the ship.

**Quartermaster**
*Category:* Inquirer and semi-specialized producer

Starting items: Armory Key and Magazine Key

**Capabilities**

- Better room rogue scans and searches for items.
- Exclusive action to check items on other players.
- Provides any specialized TPs at 2x instead of 3x.

**Player Experience: Meddling Bureaucrat**

With his keys, the Quartermaster regulates the flow of weapons and powder, and with his room scans and item checks he pushes suspicions. His semi-specialization in production makes him useful everywhere and he is the best at finding traps and hidden item.

**Sentry**
Category: Inquirer and expedition

Starting item: Spyglass

**Capabilities**

- His presence greatly increases rogue gauge fill for acolytes who have been in the same room as him during the tick of an acolyte action.
- His presence in expedition improves the odds of positive encounters.
- Super reflexes prevent all surprise attacks.

**Player Experience: Projected Focus**

The Sentry’s vigilance makes sabotage harder to hide and expeditions safer. He is not a leader by voice but by position: where he stands, inquiry pressure follows.

**Cook**
Category: Producer

Starting item: Knife

**Capabilities**

- Most efficient to produce meals.

**Player Experience: Fountain of survival**

The Cook feeds the crew more efficiently than anyone else, but every meal creates visible alliances and enemies. Because poisoning is always possible, he lives under constant suspicion.

**Engineer**
Category: Producer and overall supporter

**Capabilities**

- A whole catalogue of productions requires his specialised TP, occasionally with moderate contribution from either general or other specialised TP.
- His specialized productions may improve combats or expeditions or indirectly interact with inquiry mechanics

**Player Experience: Versatile Artisan**

The Engineer produces tools, weapons, and gadgets that shape combat, expedition and inquiry. He may regularly ask for contributions from other players but he may also focus on productions where he is mostly autonomous.

**Scholar**
Category: Elite support inquirer and producer but vulnerable fighter

**Capabilities**

- A whole catalogue of productions requires his specialised TP, often with high numbers of specialised TPs from others.
- His specialized productions often require rare items and they improve various odds during expeditions or provide occult counter-measures which drain hope.
- Weak in combat.

**Player Experience: Frail Savant**

The consumes rare resources and heavy amounts of TP from others to unlock powerful occult countermeasures or expedition boons. If protected, he can give the crew tools no one else can provide.

**Doctor**
Category: Producer and expedition support

**Capabilities**

- Medical productions require his specialised TP.
- Exclusive actions provide important clues.
- Increases the odds of survival in expeditions.
- Weak in combat.

**Player Experience: Quiet Healer**

The Doctor keeps the crew alive but at a high cost. Healing and curing require scarce resources and much TP. He can provide crucial clues through his medical knowledge, yet his role is also a magnet for suspicion, since every cure could as easily be a poison.

**Marine**
Category: Elite fighter

Starting items: Sword and Musket
**Capabilities**

- Strongest overall fighter, especially outside.
- His hope is unaffected by crew members’ deaths.

**Player Experience: Judge and Executioner**

The Marine is strongest fighter, able to decide debates by force when words fail. A loyal strike can save the crew, while a reckless one brands him a murderer.

**Brute**
Category: Fighter and support producer
**Capabilities**

- Strongest unarmed fighter.
- Higher carry capacity.

**Player Experience: Policeman and Hauler**

The Brute’s alone can easily control prisoners without weapons and carry huge loads across the ship. In calm moments he is a workhorse, in tense ones a jailer.

**Ranger**
Category: Fighter and elite expeditioner

Starting items: Knife and Musket
**Capabilities**

- Greatly improves all expedition odds.
- Very strong with in ranger combats outside.

**Player Experience: Valiant adventurer**

The Ranger skills improve survival odds during expeditions and his musket gives him reliable firepower outdoors. Reports of what he saw and did away from the ship may become flashpoints for debate.

**Sapper**
Category: Fighter and support expeditioner

Starting items: Pistol
**Capabilities**

- Greatest strength with explosives in combat and in expeditions
- His specialised TP are required for the production of explosives and a few crucial items.

**Player Experience : Ticking Bomberman**

The Sapper can clear the way for loyal success or plunge everyone into danger. Usually quiet, he becomes the center of attention whenever dynamite or grenades are used — a hero if loyal, a prime suspect if not.
