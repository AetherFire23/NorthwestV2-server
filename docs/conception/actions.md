**Actions**

**Inquiry**

Universal actions

**Carouse (Rogue action)**

- **Effect**: Both participating players use a moderate accuracy identity ping on the other (acolytes do not, they already know all crew’s loyalties). This is a high-alarm scan; loyals are likely to appear insincere. Thus, the rare loyal “sincere” pings offer a more-than-moderate clear on a player.
- **Conditions**: May only be performed in the Mess. Both players must agree to participate, and the event is public (all crew see who takes part).
- **Cost**: High TP. Each participant must consume enough liquor to become drunk. Drunkenness reduces hope instead of restoring it. Also, fatigue.
- **Ping Accuracy**: 65%.

**Interrogate**

- **Effect**: Provides a moderate accuracy (high accuracy for the Captain) rogue ping on the target. This is a low-alarm scan; filled rogue gauges will often appear unsuspicious, so the rare “suspicious” pings will be more reliable than overall accuracy.
- **Conditions**: May be performed on any single player. Both players must be in the same room but the target’s consent is not required. Action is public (the interrogation is seen by all), but the ping result is known only to the interrogator.
- **Cost**: 6 TP.
- **Ping Accuracy**: 70% for Captain, 60% for everyone else.

**Peruse**

- **Effect**: Provides a rogue ping based on the highest rogue gauge (either visible or secret) among all players present in the room.
- **Conditions**: Must be performed in a room. The action is public (everyone sees the audit), but only the perusing player receives the result.
- **Cost**: 8 TP.
- **Ping Accuracy**: 80% for Quartermaster, 65% for everyone else

**Search**

- **Effect**: Reveals hidden objects or traps within the current room.
- **Conditions**: Must be performed in a room. Public action — all crew present see that the room is being searched, though only the searching player receives the exact result. Anyone may fake finding a trap.
- **Cost**: 1TP for Quartermaster, 3TP for everyone else.

**Captain actions**

Exclusive

- **Command:** Gives 2TP to target player at the cost of 1 TP for the Captain, but those 2TP must be used for a specific task within one day. These 2TP must be spent with 2TP of the target player: this provides 4TP on the task in total. Target entirely free to ignore command, but then the 2 bonus TP are wasted. **Chaplain actions**

Exclusive

**Bless**

**Effect:** Grants the target resistance against hope loss for the next three days. Prevents decline but does not restore lost hope.

**Conditions:** May be performed on any single player. The action is public (all crew see who is blessed).

**Cost:** 3 TP.

**Confession**

**Effect:** Restores a low amount of hope to the target player. In addition, the Chaplain has high accuracy rogue scan on the target. This is a high-alarm scan; a filled rogue gauge will almost never ping, which makes the non-rogue “repentant” pings very reliable that the target’s rogue gauge is low/empty.

**Conditions:** May be performed on any single player. Requires the target’s consent and a contribution of TP. Both players must be in the same room. The act is public (all crew see who confesses), but the repentance ping is known only to the Chaplain.

**Cost:** 6 TP for the Chaplain and 4 TP for the target.

**Ping Accuracy:** To be set

**Catechesis**

**Effect: The Chaplain** receives a moderate accuracy identity scan. This is a high-alarm scan; loyals are likely to appear insincere. Thus, the rare loyal “sincere” pings offer a more-than-moderate clear on a player.

**Conditions:** May be performed on any single player. Requires the target’s consent and a full TP contribution. Both players must be in the same room. The action is public (all crew see who undergoes catechesis), but the sincerity result is known only to the Chaplain.

**Cost:** 10 TP for both the Chaplain and the target.

**Ping Accuracy:** To be set

**Exorcism**

**Effect:** Targets a single player with a prolonged ritual.

- If the target is cursed, the curse is lifted and a demonic scream erupts, publicly audible to all.
- If the target is a acolyte, they choose between two outcomes:
  - Produce a demonic scream identical to a real curse being lifted (no true effect), or
  - Suffer a hidden permanent weakening in combat (no visible sign).
- If the target is neither cursed nor a acolyte, no effect occurs.

**Conditions:** May be performed on any single player. Consent of the target is not required. The action is public (all crew see who is being exorcised) and any demonic scream is heard by all the crew.

**Cost:** 18 TP from the Chaplain.

**Productions**

Productions are the system by which players create new items. Every item made during the game — whether a quick meal, a tool, a weapon, a medical remedy, or a complex upgrade — is produced through this mechanic. A production may be simple or demanding: it might require only one player, a single stage, and a few TP, or it might span several stages, involve specialized TP from multiple roles, and leave unfinished items lying around for others to complete.

Items that exist only as starting equipment (such as keys, flour, or gunpowder) or that can only be found during expeditions (such as meat, relics, or scrolls) are not productions.

- **Initiation**: Any player holding the required items in the correct room may start a production. When initiated, an unfinished version of the produced item is created in the room’s inventory. The items used to start it are locked inside this unfinished item.
- **Unfinished Items**: Unfinished items behave like normal items: they can be picked up, carried away, and left in other rooms. They visibly store both the locked items and the record of accumulated TP. It is common to see many unfinished productions lying around, especially in the Workshop.
- **Stages**: Each production is divided into one or several stages, each with its own TP quota and room requirement.
  - Any number of players may contribute TP to a stage.
  - Contributions are permanent: TP spent on a production remains bound to it until the end of the game. There is no decay on TPs spent on productions.
  - When one stage is complete, the next becomes immediately available.
- **Specialized TP**: TP requirements are either general or specialized. If specialized TPs are provided by non-specialists, their cost is tripled (doubled for the Quartermaster).
- **Cancellation**: Any player may cancel any production in their current room. This destroys the unfinished item and immediately releases the locked items back into the room’s inventory. However, all TP invested in the production is permanently lost.
- **Completion**: When the final stage is satisfied, the production resolves instantly. The locked items are consumed, and the finished item is automatically picked up by the player who provided the last TP.

Engineer productions focus on **technical craft** and generally lean on **moderate, occasional contributions of general TP** from other players.

- **Weapons & Tools**: Tangible combat and expedition gear. Distribution is always political, since every new weapon changes the balance of power.
- **Inquiry Gadgets**: Do not provide scans directly, but **modify or influence other players’ scans** (scope, reliability, shielding).

**Acolyte actions**

All evil deeds in Northwest are unified as **acolyte actions**.
Mechanically, they follow the same rules: they consume gauges, leave traces, and create suspect pools.

For thematic clarity, the action list distinguishes two skins:

- **Sabotage** → mundane treacheries of steel, poison, and fire. These fill the **rogue gauge**.
- **Occult** → supernatural intrusions tied to Abaddon’s power. These consume the **occult gauge**, and sometimes fill the rogue gauge as well.

This distinction is **flavor only**. No rule outside the acolyte action list ever refers to “sabotage” or “occult.”

Acolyte actions always cost **no TP**. Their cost is entirely in rogue/occult gauges and the suspect blocks they generate.

**Sabotage**

**Poison**

- **Conditions**:
Requires presence in the galley, or carrying ingredients/meals at some point. More helpers in food circulation = broader suspect pool.
- **Effects**:
Direct *health damage* + lingering *combat weakness*.
*Doctor’s antidote* cures both.

**Occult**

**Scrawl**

The acolyte secretly inscribes one of the following blasphemous phrases on the walls of a chosen room:

- *“Do you hear Abaddon breathes?”*
- *“Unmake rotten creation.”*
- *“Your pride belongs in the Abyss.”*

**Hidden State:** The scrawl begins hidden.
**Discovery:** The first player to search this room discovers it and immediately suffers **–10 Hope**.
**Ongoing Effect:** Once revealed, the scrawl erodes morale, inflicting **–1 Hope per tick to all crew**.
**Removal:** Erasing the blasphemy requires a **collective 10 TP** spent on cleaning.

**Restriction:** Each phrase may only exist in one place at a time. Until a phrase is found and removed, it cannot be scrawled again elsewhere.

**Disturb**

**Effect:** Fuels an ongoing unrest event into one of three channels — *Loathing, Guilt,* or *Isolation*. The acting acolyte selects both the channel and a corresponding piece of flavor text. The chosen channel determines the distinct Hope loss and thematic effects on the crew. The other acolyte may veto the attempt; if vetoed, the action is canceled (occult gauge is not spent) and the actor may not attempt another disturb action until the next event.

**Conditions:** Only one disturb action in effect for each unrest event. Each acolyte may attempt this disturb action only once before each unrest event.

**Cost:** Moderate occult gauge, small increase of secret rogue gauge.

**Guilt (Channel of Unrest)**
**Theme:** Moral scandal corrodes trust. Crew members feel judged, condemned, and unworthy. Hope loss is severe unless the crew confronts the charges.

**Crew reactions:**

- **Scrutiny** – The Chaplain conducts repeated *Confession* actions on different players until several suspicious pings are obtained. Each iteration deepens mistrust and **erodes the whole crew’s Hope**.
- **Redemption** – Instead of pursuing scrutiny, the Chaplain may depart on a prolonged expedition. If successful, this resolves the crisis of Guilt, though his absence hinders inquiry and survival tasks.

**If the Chaplain is dead:**

- *Scrutiny* may be replaced with repeated *Interrogate* actions, but a **much larger number of suspicious pings** is required.
- *Redemption* may be replaced with a collective expedition involving **at least 75% of surviving crew members**.

**Loathing (Channel of Unrest)**
**Theme:** Hatred and disgust spread through the crew. Members come to see one another as tainted, unworthy, or burdensome. Hope loss is severe unless the crew channels the hatred onto a target or into renunciation.

**Crew reactions:**

- **Scapegoat** – A new player must be jailed for a time. The chosen player must be one of the **production roles**, ensuring the crew loses a contributor.
- **Austerity** – Instead of scapegoating, the crew may prohibit most production actions for a while. This self-denial blunts the hatred but sharply reduces efficiency.

**Isolation (Channel of Unrest)**
**Theme:** Estrangement spreads through the crew. Members withdraw from one another, trust frays, and cooperation falters. Hope loss is severe unless the crew reacts collectively or individually.

**Crew reactions:**

- **Feast** – A gathering is organized in the Mess where the crew eats and fraternizes. This requires shared participation and food resources, but restores cohesion and mitigates the effects of Isolation.
- **Solitude** – Alternatively, players may avoid sharing rooms, productions, and expeditions with others. This reduces efficiency and inquiry opportunities, but prevents despair from spreading further.
